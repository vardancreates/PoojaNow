# Getting PoojaNow live on Netlify

I can't log into your Netlify account or push this for you directly — but this folder
is ready to go live in under a minute using Netlify's drag-and-drop deploy.

## Fastest way (no account needed to try it)

1. Go to **https://app.netlify.com/drop**
2. Drag this whole folder (`poojanow-site`) into the drop zone
3. Netlify gives you a live URL instantly (something like `random-name-123.netlify.app`)

That link is live on the internet immediately — you can share it right away to test on
your phone or send to anyone.

## If you want it under your own Netlify account (recommended before sharing widely)

1. Create a free account at **https://app.netlify.com/signup** (or log in if you have one)
2. Go to **Sites → Add new site → Deploy manually**
3. Drag the `poojanow-site` folder in the same way
4. Once deployed, go to **Site settings → Change site name** to pick a custom subdomain,
   e.g. `poojanow.netlify.app`

## Later: connecting your own domain (poojanow.co.in)

Once you're ready, Netlify lets you connect a custom domain for free:
**Site settings → Domain management → Add a domain** → follow the DNS steps it gives you
(you'll add a couple of records wherever you bought poojanow.co.in from).

No rush on this — the free `.netlify.app` link works perfectly fine for testing and
showing people the prototype.
