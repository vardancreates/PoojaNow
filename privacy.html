Your Privacy

What we collect

When you sign in with Google and place an order on PoojaNow, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and delivery address
  • Your exact delivery location (GPS coordinates), used only to help our delivery person find your doorstep
  • Your Google account email, used to save your details and order history so you don't have to re-enter them next time
  • Your order history — what you've ordered, when, and its status

Who can see this

Only the PoojaNow team can see your order details and delivery information. We don't sell, share, or hand over your information to any third party for advertising or any other purpose.

Where it's stored

Your information is stored securely using Google Firebase, the same infrastructure used by many apps you already trust. Product photos are hosted via Cloudinary. No payment card details are ever stored by us — for UPI payments, you pay directly through your own UPI app.

Your choices

You can request that we delete your account and order history at any time by contacting us at privacy@poojanow.example.
